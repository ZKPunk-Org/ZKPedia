# Exploring Zero-Knowledge Proofs

> Author: **Yu Guo @ SECBIT** (郭宇), founder of [SECBIT Labs](https://secbit.io/), <https://github.com/sec-bit>
>
> Original (Chinese): <https://github.com/sec-bit/learning-zkp/>
>
> Published here with attribution. If you spot a mistranslation, please [open a pull request](https://github.com/ZKPunk-Org/ZKPedia).

A five-part introduction to what zero-knowledge proofs actually are, written for readers who want the reasoning rather than the metaphor. It starts from what it means to prove something at all, then works through simulation, knowledge extraction, random challenges, and trusted setup.

All five parts are available in English. The Chinese originals are in the [Chinese edition](https://learn.zkpunk.pro/zkp-intro/toc.html).
